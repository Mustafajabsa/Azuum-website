Powerful Modules for Your Business

Explore the comprehensive modules available in Azuum's form management system. Each module is designed to streamline your workflow and boost productivity.

Dashboard

Get a comprehensive overview of your form activities with real-time analytics, statistics, and insights. Monitor form submissions, user engagement, and system performance all in one centralized location.

  • Real-time analytics and insights
  • Activity monitoring and tracking
  • Performance metrics dashboard

Storage

Manage your files and documents with secure cloud storage. Organize, upload, and access all your form attachments and media files with advanced security features and easy sharing capabilities.

  • Secure cloud storage solution
  • File organization and management
  • Easy sharing and collaboration

Forms

Create and customize professional forms with our intuitive drag-and-drop builder. Add various field types, implement validation rules, and design forms that match your brand identity perfectly.

  • Drag-and-drop form builder
  • Custom field types and validation
  • Brand customization options

Filled Forms

View, manage, and analyze all submitted forms in one place. Export data, generate reports, and gain valuable insights from user responses with powerful filtering and search capabilities.

  • Centralized form submissions
  • Data export and reporting
  • Advanced filtering and search

Settings

Configure your system preferences, manage user accounts, and customize your workspace. Control security settings, notifications, and integrations to tailor Azuum to your specific needs.

  • User account management
  • Security and privacy settings
  • Integration configurations
