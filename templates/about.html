Our Story

From a simple idea to a comprehensive enterprise solution

2019 - The Beginning

Founded by enterprise software veterans who saw the gap between basic form tools and complex enterprise systems. Started with a simple question: "Why can't form management be both powerful and easy to use?"

2021 - Growth

Launched our enterprise platform with advanced features like role-based access, comprehensive analytics, and API integrations. Served our first 100 organizations and received overwhelming positive feedback.

2024 - Innovation

Now serving 500+ organizations globally with cutting-edge features, AI-powered insights, and industry-leading security. Continuously innovating to meet the evolving needs of modern businesses.
